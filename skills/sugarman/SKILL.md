# Sugarman (slippery slide) reviewer

Paraphrased from Joseph Sugarman's well-known teaching in *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*. Not quotations.

## Rules (paraphrased)

1. The only job of the first line is to get the second line read.
2. The first line should be short and easy to read.
3. Plant curiosity: open a loop the reader wants closed.
4. Remove every bump: confusing words, filler openers ("This also means", "So,"), context the reader doesn't have.
5. Close the loop by the end. A payoff, not a shrug.

## Steps

Read line by line. Mark each line where a reader could stop scrolling, and why.

## Scoring rubric (0-10)

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0-2 | First line is filler or context-dependent; nothing pulls forward. |
| 3-4 | Some pull, but a bump early on. |
| 5-6 | Decent first line, loop opened but not paid off (or vice versa). |
| 7-8 | Strong first line, smooth read, payoff lands. |
| 9-10 | Impossible to stop reading; payoff makes you want to reread or reply. |

## Rewrite instruction

- Rewrite the first line first. Under ~10 words if possible.
- Cut filler openers. Make the post stand alone without the quoted post.
- Return: `stop_points` (list), `score`, `one_line_reason`, `top_fix`, `rewrite`.
