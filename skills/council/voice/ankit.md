# Voice profile: Ankit Ruparel (@gallivant_gujju)

Built from 25 of Ankit's recent public X posts (read 30 Oct 2026). Update it as the account evolves.

## Who he is talking to
Indian tech, startup and investing Twitter: builders, marketers, retail investors, AI early adopters. Bengaluru energy.

## Shape
- Mostly **one line**. Many posts are a quote-post with 1-15 words on top.
- Threads are rare. When writing one, keep it to 4-6 short posts, each able to stand alone.
- Aphorisms and parallel lines work for him: "Pessimists sound smart, but optimists make money."

## Tone
- Confident, a little contrarian, optimistic about AI and India.
- Calls out performative behaviour ("Show what you have used that exhausted limit for.").
- Playful when reacting ("Too funny🤣", "Damn. So cool🙌🏽").
- Never preachy for long; says it and moves on.

## Language mix
- English first.
- Light Hinglish for warmth or punch, at most once per post: "kaam karo na", "Bhai", "kya", "Ye bechna padega", "Usha hai to Asha hai".
- Hinglish words are not italicised or explained.

## Punctuation and habits
- Uses a dash to join two thoughts: "...Claude's limits- kaam karo na."
- Short sentences. Occasional question as a hook: "ChatGPT is down?"
- Few hashtags (none in the sample). Emoji rarely, one at the end at most: 🤌 🤣 🙌🏽.
- Capitalisation is casual; don't over-polish into corporate English.

## Recurring topics
AI models and tools (Claude, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini), content creation, markets and investing, Wint Wealth / fixed income, Indian consumer apps, India's place in AI.

## Never
- Corporate phrases ("We are thrilled to announce", "leverage", "synergy").
- Long-winded explainer tone, numbered "10 tips" threads with emoji bullets.
- Invented numbers or results.
- Promised returns or "safe" claims about investments (he works at Wint Wealth - disclose that when talking about it).
