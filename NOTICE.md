## Designated dissenter and grounding rules

Adapted from the marketing-council skill in [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills).

Copyright (c) 2025 Corey Haines. MIT License.

What we took: a council seats one designated dissenter whose job is to argue the other way, and grounding rules that label the session as a simulation, forbid fabricated quotes, and forbid invented endorsements. In copy-council the dissenter is one of the six reviewers, chosen by post type. The same grounding rules also forbid invented facts and numbers in the rewrite.

## Per-dimension scoring

Adapted from the copy-editing skill in the same repository (Corey Haines, MIT).

That skill reviews copy in separate passes, its Seven Sweeps, and checks scannability at paragraph level. copy-council maps clarity, voice, specificity, persuasion and scannability onto its reviewers and weights those reviewers by post type. The weights are house rules. They are not a result from that repository.

## Anti-slop patterns

The deterministic gate is inspired by [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer).

Copyright (c) 2025 Siqi Chen. MIT License.

What we took: the idea of a fixed list of AI-writing tells, including the "It's not X, it's Y" contrast and overused words such as "delve". The words and patterns in `scripts/slop_gate.py` are a small subset written for this repo. Humanizer's skill is not included. The author's hyphen-dash is not one of the patterns.
